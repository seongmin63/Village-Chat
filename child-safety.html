Child Safety Standards

Village Chat ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to maintaining a safe environment and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form.

Prohibited Content and Conduct

Village Chat strictly prohibits:

  • Any content that sexually exploits or abuses minors (CSAM)
  • Grooming, solicitation, or any predatory behavior toward minors
  • Sharing, requesting, or distributing any material that depicts the sexual exploitation of children

Age Requirement

Village Chat is intended for users aged 17 and older. Users who are found to be under the required age will have their accounts terminated.

Reporting Mechanisms

Users can report child safety concerns directly within the app. Reports are reviewed promptly. We also encourage users to report concerns to the relevant national authorities:

  • Korea: Korean National Police Agency (police.go.kr) or the Korea Internet & Security Agency (KISA)
  • USA: National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) – CyberTipline at cybertipline.org
  • International: INHOPE (inhope.org)

Reporting to Authorities

Village Chat complies with all applicable child safety laws. We report confirmed CSAM to the relevant regional and national authorities, including NCMEC where required, and cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

Enforcement

Any user found to have violated these standards will be permanently banned from Village Chat. Evidence of illegal activity will be reported to law enforcement without delay.
